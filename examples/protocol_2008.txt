WOODLAND 2008 — FICTIONAL TRAINING PROTOCOL

This is fictional training material, not an actual study or a claim about real locations.

Three inherited tables: events, obs, taxa. event_id in obs identifies the survey
record in events. taxon_code links an observation to a taxa row. The local
dictionary was incomplete; do not assign identities to unknown taxon codes.

Field n: number of individuals visually detected during the recorded survey.
The code NA means the count was not recorded. A literal zero is a recorded
zero count and must not become missing. Other sentinel codes were not documented.

Survey effort is recorded in minutes in effort_min. An event with no observation
rows is not proof that no organisms were detected; records may be missing.

Observer names were removed from this fictional example. Species labels and
coordinates are illustrative only. Some coordinates are intentionally questionable.
The coordinate datum did not survive. Do not assume WGS84.

Dates with slashes could be day/month or month/day; the notebook convention is lost.
The cryptic field code has no surviving definition. Keep it Unknown.
There is no evidence supporting automatic duplicate removal.
